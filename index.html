About

I am co-founder and Vice President of CNA A-TRES (Matanzas, Cuba), the cooperative that pioneered plastic lumber (“ecomadera”) production in Cuba from post-industrial and post-consumer plastics, with no virgin resin. I have also taken part in its aluminum production line, including casting.

From 2022 to 2025 I led, on behalf of A-TRES, a South-South cooperation project under the GEF Small Grants Programme (UNDP) that transferred this technology to communities in Belize and Honduras. Based in Cancún, I now work on circular economy and marine litter projects.
